# What `silero_vad.proof.json` is — and is not

`silero_vad.proof.json` is **schema and pipeline evidence**, not deployment
evidence. Do not cite it as proof about any deployed Silero VAD model.

## What it is

A `CertificateBundle` (format version 6, `nn-verify`) with 7 certificates,
generated by the self-contained two-step chain:

1. `cargo run -p nn-verify --example verify_all` → `nn_verify.proof.json`
2. `cargo run -p nn-verify --example generate_proof_bundle -- --model silero_vad --output silero_vad.proof.json`

It exercises and pins down:

- the certificate **schema** (versioning, serialization round-trip),
- the **bound-propagation pipeline** (IBP/CROWN layer bounds through a
  35-layer composed graph),
- the **checker** (`check_bundle`) and the structural-validation invariants
  enforced by `crates/nn-verify/tests/helpers/validate_silero_vad_proof_bundle.rs`.

The 6 kernel-level certificates (`sigmoid`, `sigmoid_wide`, `relu`,
`relu_wide`, `tanh_act`, `tanh_act_wide`) are bound certificates for
standalone activation kernels; they are weight-free and stand on their own.

## What it is not — the `silero_vad_full` record

The `silero_vad_full` composition certificate was computed on a graph with
**synthetic weights**, not real Silero VAD weights. See
`crates/nn-verify/examples/verify_all/model_configs.rs`:

- `build_silero_vad_model()` (line ~113) builds the post-STFT graph shape
  (4 conv+ReLU encoder blocks → LSTM → ReLU → linear → sigmoid), and
- `silero_vad_bindings()` (line ~146) binds **all** conv, LSTM, and output
  weights to the uniform constant `0.01f32` and all biases to `0.0`.

Consequently the `silero_vad_full` output bounds (≈ [0.500, 0.726]) are
properties of that synthetic-weight graph only.

In addition, the record carries **no deployment binding** of any kind:

- no hash of real model weights (`source_hash` binds the certificate to the
  kernel/graph *definition*, not to weights or a binary),
- no compiled-binary hash or link to a shipped artifact,
- no numeric-profile record (no fp32/fp16 execution-profile comparison
  against a reference implementation).

## Usage rules

- **May** be cited as: evidence that the certificate schema, bound
  propagation, checker, and bundle round-trip work end-to-end on a
  full-size composed graph.
- **Must not** be cited as: deployment evidence, model-quality evidence, or
  evidence about the behavior of any real Silero VAD model or binary.

The bundle's top-level `"provenance"` field carries the same warning
machine-readably:

```json
"provenance": "synthetic-weights schema evidence — not deployment evidence"
```

A future deployment-grade bundle must bind real weights (weight hash),
the compiled artifact (binary hash), and a numeric profile before it may be
cited for deployment.
